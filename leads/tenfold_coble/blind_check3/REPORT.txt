C7  by hand
 (a) PASS. h^5.h^5 = 3. If h^5 = k*b with b integral then 3 = k^2 (b.b), so
     k = +-1: h^5 is indivisible. If [W] = q h^5 with q rational then q is an
     integer (indivisible vector in a torsion-free lattice), and
     deg W = [W].h^5 = 3q. So degree not divisible by 3 forces [W] outside
     Q.h^5. Assumed: X smooth, so H^10(X,Z) is torsion-free with an integral
     intersection form (Lefschetz, Poincare duality).
 (b) PASS. c_5 of the normal bundle: coefficient of h^5 in (1+h)^6/(1+3h) =
     6 - 45 + 180 - 405 + 486 - 243 = -21 = (1-64)/3. Two 5-planes meeting in
     a plane: excess bundle of rank 2 with total class (1+h)^3/(1+3h), h^2
     coefficient 9 - 9 + 3 = 3 = (1+8)/3. h^5.P = 1.
       (h^5+P1+P2)^2            = 3 - 21 - 21 + 2 + 2 + 6      = -29
       (3h^5-P1-P2)^2           = 27 - 21 - 21 - 6 - 6 + 6     = -21
       (h^5+P1+P2).(3h^5-P1-P2) = 9 - 1 - 1 + 3 + 21 - 3 + 3 - 3 + 21 = 49
     Sum 4h^5; degrees 3+1+1 = 5 and 9-1-1 = 7. Assumed: a smooth cubic
     tenfold with two 5-planes meeting exactly in a plane (the Fermat cubic
     has such pairs).
 (c) Formula: re-derived independently by blowing up the nodes of X' inside
     X. With mu = multiplicity and L = class of the projectivised tangent
     cone in the exceptional quadric Q of dimension 2(m-1):
        Z.Z' (X) = (Z cap H').(Z' cap H') (X'') + sum_p [mu mu' - 2 (L.L')_Q]
     and for L = aA + bB, n = a - b this bracket is -(-1)^(m-1) n n'. So the
     stated formula is correct, sign included. Sanity checks on linear
     spaces: plane in a cubic fourfold 3 = -1 + 4; P^3 in a cubic sixfold
     -5 = 3 - 8; P^4: 11 = -5 + 16; P^5: -21 = 11 - 32.
     Arithmetic with n = +1 for Z, Z_2 and -1 for Z', Z'_2:
        Z'.Z' = 13 - 14 + 28 - 56 = -29   (levels: -1, 27, -29)
        Z.Z   = 21 - 14 + 28 - 56 = -21   (levels: 7, 35, -21)
        Z.Z'  = 7 + 14 - 28 + 56  = 49    (levels: 21, -7, 49)
     These agree with (b): degrees 5 and 7, squares -29 and -21, product 49.
     Independent consistency: Z + Z' is the complete intersection of class
     4h^5, and 48 = -21 + 2*49 - 29, 28 = -21 + 49, 20 = 49 - 29; the same
     three identities hold at every level of the ladder.
     Agreement of numbers shows equal Gram matrices of <h^5, Z'> and
     <h^5, h^5+P1+P2> (determinant -112); it does not identify the classes.
     Extra computations I made for the ladder [(32003,1), (31991,2)]:
        cubic 8-fold f restricted to {l_a = 0} in P^9: smooth;
        its section t_2 = 0 (cubic 7-fold in P^8): Jacobian scheme degree 28;
        further section l_b = 0 (cubic 6-fold in P^7): smooth;
        its section t_1 = 0 (F on P^6): degree 14; F on P^5: smooth.
        R/I has projective dimension 3 for Z, Z_2, Z', Z'_2, so all four are
        arithmetically Cohen-Macaulay and the lengths of C6 are intersection
        numbers.
     What is assumed or not computed:
      1. Self-intersections are computed as Z.Z_2 and Z'.Z'_2, so Z ~ Z_2 and
         Z' ~ Z'_2 must be homologous in X. Reason offered: both pairs are
         members of connected families (3-spaces in G(3,6), hyperplanes in
         k^6). Not checked by computation.
      2. The local classes are needed at the nodes, which are not rational

         extend to all nodes because S is smooth and connected, Z and Z_2 are

      3. A smooth cubic tenfold X with hyperplane section the 56-nodal f = 0
         was not constructed; f + t_3 * (general quadric) is smooth by a
         Bertini argument in characteristic 0. NOT COMPUTED.
      4. Everything numerical is mod p; no lift to characteristic 0 was done.
