8.2 But this does NOT characterise delta. The functionals on forms of degree k+1 killing J(F) and s form
    a space of dimension dim (C[x]/(J(f),Q))_(k+1). In case (a) this is 14 (computed at both primes; it is

    Facts I can add:
     * ker delta = (I_Z + (Q)) in degree k+1 (short argument: P/Q restricted to Z is a form of degree k-1
       iff P - QR is in I_Z), and this has codimension exactly 1 when the defect is 1. So
       I = Gorenstein ideal of the socle-degree-(k+1) part of C[x]/(I_Z + (Q)), and a functional killing
       I_Z, Q is unique up to scalar.
     * Why the class should kill I_Z: a cubic g vanishing on the nodes is a first-order equisingular

 (i) The residue argument is right: P omega/(Psi_1 Psi_2) is a meromorphic 2-form with poles on two curves
     meeting transversally at Z, the sum of its point residues P(p) omega(p)/Jac(p) vanishes, all coefficients
     are nonzero, and by T2 this is the only relation. With K_S of order 2 the same theorem applies to
     s/(Psi_1 Psi_2), s in H^0(K_S+4h), and says nothing about quartics.
     Bookkeeping: under a change of chart the coefficients of the relation scale with weight e = n/2 - 1 and
     the affine Hessian determinant with weight -(n-2) (adj Hess = c(z) z z^T, c homogeneous of degree n-2),
     so g^2 D has weight 0 and the statement is chart-independent. A principal (n-1)-minor has no such
     meaning. Local reason in the T2 setting: in a splitting N + T_S + (w-directions) the Hessian is block
     triangular and D = det(q_N) Jac_S(Psi_1,Psi_2)^2, with det(q_N) a nowhere-zero section of K_S^(-2),
     i.e. const/omega^2. Same for curves ((b),(d): K_C = O_C(1), D = -det(q_N)(dPsi|C)^2).

     theorem for the section df of N^v(3) gives the relation sum R(p)/Jac(p) = 0 for forms of degree k-1
     (K_Sigma + det N^v(3) = O(k-1)), and the Hessian has the block form [[0,A],[A^T,C]], D = +-det(A)^2.

     by T2 (p_g = 0) or by Q11, not by the sign heuristic.

Q11. K3 cover of the 64 Enriques nodes                                                           PASS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Parity argument: for invariant equations and an invariant bordering linear form, grad F_i(iota p) =
iota grad F_i(p), so J(iota p) = det(iota) J(p) = -J(p). The unique Cayley-Bacharach relation is therefore
an anti-invariant functional on the 128-dimensional space of functions; it vanishes on the 64 invariant
functions and cuts a hyperplane in the 64 anti-invariant ones. Restriction of octics is equivariant, so
invariant octics have rank 64 and anti-invariant ones 63. Invariant octics are the quartics in x_i x_j,
y_i y_j; anti-invariant octics give all of H^0(K_S+4h) because the K3 is projectively normal. Correct.
q11.m2, MOD 32003 and 31991: equations of degrees 2,2,2,4,4; length 128; reduced; HF
1,6,18,38,64,90,110,122,127,128; J anti-invariant (checked symbolically, degree 9);
ranks: all octics 127, invariant 64, anti-invariant 63, pull-backs of quartics of P^8 64.
Downstairs: length 64, HF(4) = 64.

Q9.  OTHER REMARKS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 Is the overall conclusion supported?
    Supported by what I verified: K3 -> 56 nodes, defect 1; abelian (Coble) -> 72 nodes, defect 1;
    Enriques -> 64 nodes, defect 0, and no 5-fold smooth along S can exist; bielliptic (this type, both
    linearisations) -> not a singular locus of any cubic.
    NOT supported as stated: "K3 <-> coefficient +1 and abelian <-> coefficient -1 is forced". What is
    forced is a discriminant (-112, -144), conditionally on a suitable Z. Nothing I was given places these
    classes in the lattice <h^5,P1,P2>. "Enriques and bielliptic give no class" should read "this
    construction gives no class": defect 0 says the nodal section carries no extra class, not that the
    tenfold has none.
9.2 Abelian surfaces of type (1,9) in P^8 (degree 18, linearly normal) are not covered. N = N^v(3) imposes
    no numerical condition on a surface beyond 2K = 0, so I cannot exclude them. If one were a singular locus
    it would give the same -144. Not checked.
9.3 Non-linearly-normal surfaces are not covered by T4(i): a projected K3 has degree >= 16. 2K = 0 still
    holds, T2 needs 4-normality, and the mod 3 argument only removes degrees = 1 mod 3. Not checked.
9.4 Minor points: the Pfaffian Jacobian ideal needs saturating (Q2); I_S + (two quadrics) is not saturated
    in the Coble case, so "conditions" must be read on the saturation (73 against 71 in degree 4); the
    reducedness of the 72 Coble nodes and the smoothness of the Coble surface were not verified by me.
