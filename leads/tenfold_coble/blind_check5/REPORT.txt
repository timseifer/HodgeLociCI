     in the 924-dimensional space of period polynomials (all primitive (5,5)-classes of X_0) those whose period
     equations vanish to order n along the T7 arc form a space of dimension 704, 484, 264, 44, 1 for n = 1..5, and
     the 1-dimensional space at n = 5 is the line of kappa. Along the symmetric arc F_0 + tau*M_xi the corresponding
     dimensions are 707, 625, 571, 489, 462, ..., 325 at n = 14 (still decreasing). So T6 is a weak test of kappa
     (consistent with T5 item 8, and F_x has many more symmetries than Gamma'); T7 is the discriminating one.
  2. Special plane x_1 + x_2 = 0: there the first-order equations of delta vanish (T1), and MOD p1, p2 (2 random
     choices each, also with x_0 = 0 or x = (a,0,0)) ALL period equations of all three delta's vanish to order 12
     along F_0 + tau*M_x. On that plane each single plane class seems to persist, so tests of kappa with such x are
     uninformative. (For x_0 = 0, x_1 + x_2 = 0 the three planes lie in X_x: M_x restricted to P_z is
     z*(x_0*sum y_(2i) q_i^2 + (x_1+x_2)*sum_{i<j} y_(i+j) q_i q_j).)
  3. Choices that make parts of T7 vacuous: lam_1 = 0 makes (i),(ii),(iii) equal to the true arc; xi_0 xi_1 xi_2 = 0
     kills (ii); h_0(xi) = 0 removes the leading term of a; phi_k = 0 kills (iv); xi_1 + xi_2 = 0 weakens (v);
     N <= 6 cannot see (iii). My random data avoid all of these (checked by the variants failing as listed).
  4. Small integer x are often NOT in U. EXACT (M2) at the 729 integer points of [-4,4]^3: G_x (hence X_x) is
     singular at 272 of them, exactly those on one of the 36 hyperplanes  w^e0 x_0 + w^e1 x_1 + w^e2 x_2 = -3
     (e in (Z/3)^3) or x_i = -3 w^k (w a primitive cube root of 1). E.g. x = (1,2,3) is singular; x = (3,15,18) is
     smooth. I did not prove that these 36 hyperplanes are the whole discriminant.
  5. The formula for a in T7 is exactly what makes the P^8-section special: EXACT over QQ at 4 rational (lam, x), the
     cubic a*h_lam(y)+h_lam(p)+h_lam(q)+M_x is singular along a surface of degree 18 with Hilbert polynomial 9t^2

     NOT compute any such class or compare it with kappa.
  6. G_x has one more symmetry than those in Gamma': the block rotation t (y_k -> p_(-k), p_i -> q_i, q_j -> y_(-j))
     fixes G_x and F_x for all x but does not preserve the set of the three planes. I used it in the proof of T4.
