Hand argument, checked step by step.

     rank-1 configurations (e.g. A = u a^T, B = u b^T, C = v c^T forces v || u or a || b || c).

=====================================================================================================================
(a) Does smoothness force the dimension and regular-sequence statements?  YES.
    On Z0 := {l1 = l2 = 0, rank A <= 1, rank B <= 1} the cofactors of A and B vanish, so dF = Q1 dl1 + Q2 dl2 there and
    Sing(X) contains Z0 n {Q1 = Q2 = 0}. Every component of Z0 has dimension >= 9 - 4 - 4 = 1 and Z0 is nonempty; if a
    component had dimension >= 2 it would meet {Q1 = Q2 = 0}. (If l1, l2 were dependent, Z0 would have dimension >= 2: so
    smoothness also forces independence.) Hence Z0 is a curve and Q1, Q2 have no common zero on it. Then
    codim I_2(A) = codim I_2(B) = 4 modulo (l1, l2) (their sum has codim 8 and codimensions are subadditive), both
    quotients are Gorenstein determinantal rings with h-vector 1,4,1, they meet properly so the tensor product is
    Cohen-Macaulay and Gorenstein of dimension 2 with h-vector (1+4t+t^2)^2, and Q1, Q2 is a system of parameters, hence
    a regular sequence. S/I is Artinian Gorenstein with Hilbert function 1,10,35,52,35,10,1 at EVERY smooth member.
    (The same count shows T5 has the expected dimension at every smooth member.)

(b) Does "same Hilbert function + continuity" give Ann(delta) = I at special members?  The conclusion is right, the
    stated reason is not sufficient. A correct argument:
      1. I_0 is contained in Ann(delta_0): elements of (I_0)_6 extend to elements of (I_t)_6, on which the period functional
         of the flat class delta_t vanishes for general t; pass to the limit. (This needs no Hilbert function.)
      2. delta_0 is not 0, because delta is a flat section over the connected set of smooth members and is nonzero
         somewhere. (Independent confirmation: D_extra.py computes T3.T3 = 15 on the sixfold by Chern classes, so
         delta6^2 = -12 and, by the cone formula of claim E, delta^2 = -48.)
      3. S/I_0 is Gorenstein with socle in degree 6 and S/Ann(delta_0) is a quotient of it that is nonzero in degree 6;
         a proper quotient of an Artinian Gorenstein algebra kills the socle; so the two are equal.
    What carries the step is the Gorenstein property at the special member (from (a)), not the Hilbert function.
    "General member" must mean a general point of the SAME irreducible parameter space that contains the special member.
    Claim C tests only the sub-family with A, B in x0..x7. I therefore also ran a member of the big family (A, B in all 12
    variables): C_general.m2 / C_general_1.out: S/I has 1,10,35,52,35,10,1, socle 1 in degree 6, Z0 is a curve of degree 36

    variants T(alpha,beta) (a pair of rows or of columns of A, the same for B) all have primitive class +-delta (two row

(c) Does the conclusion need a hypothesis on the representation (l, Q, A, B)?

CLAIM E   (the cone map)
=====================================================================================================================
Construction: SOUND. E_Z = blow-up of the cone along the vertex line is a P^2-bundle p: E_Z -> Z, smooth of dimension 8,
with q: E_Z -> X. cone := q_* p^* : H^6(Z,Z) -> H^10(X,Z) is a morphism of Hodge structures of type (2,2), maps [V] to
[cone V] (q is birational on p^-1(V)), h^3 to h^5 (cone over a linear section is a linear section), preserves degree, and
is defined in families over the connected set of (c, l, Q) with X smooth (X smooth forces Z smooth and Q1, Q2 without
common zero on the vertex line). Hence {c + l1 Q1 + l2 Q2 : c in the Hodge locus of gamma} lies in the Hodge locus of
cone(gamma). One direction only: the Hodge locus of cone(gamma) can be larger.

One-step formula, proved for all classes (degree d, vertex a point v, Z of dimension 2k, X of dimension 2k+2):
   (cone a, cone b)_X = (1 - d)(a, b)_Z + deg(a) deg(b);   for d = 3:  -2(a,b) + deg(a)deg(b).
Proof sketch: blow up X at v; the strict transform of the cone is E (a P^1-bundle over Z) meeting the exceptional
P^(2k+1) transversally along the section sigma = Z; with xi = q^*h = eta + sigma and N_E = xi - d sigma (multiplicity d
at v), q^* q_* p^*b = p^*b eta + p^*((1-d) b + deg(b) h^k) sigma; pair with p^*a.
Checks: d = 2, 3 with k = 0 (two or three lines through a point of a quadric or cubic surface); lines and conics on a
cubic surface going to planes and quadric cones in a cubic fourfold (-1 -> 3, 0 -> 4); linear spaces: with
(1 - (-2)^(m+1))/3 the identity -2 * value(k,m) + 1 = value(k+1,m+1) holds for all k <= 7, m = -1..k (D_extra.py).
Two steps: (cone a, cone b) = 4(a,b) - deg(a) deg(b); the "correction" is -deg(a)deg(b), zero on primitive classes.
Checked on linear spaces: 4 * value(k,m) - 1 = value(k+2,m+2). Signs agree with Hodge-Riemann (6-fold and 10-fold negative
on primitive (k,k) classes, 8-fold positive).
Caveat on "applied twice": a general X = c + l1 Q1 + l2 Q2 is NOT an iterated one-step cone (Q1 involves both vertex
coordinates; X n {l2 = 0} is then not a cone over an eightfold). The factorisation holds at members where Q1 does not
involve the second vertex coordinate and the intermediate eightfold is smooth (e.g. Fermat); the formula then extends to
all members because the cone map is flat and the parameter space is connected. This sentence should be in the write-up.
