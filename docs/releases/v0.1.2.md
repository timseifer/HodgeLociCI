# HodgeLociCI v0.1.2 (25 November 2026): release notes

**What this is.** A snapshot of the HodgeLociCI Macaulay2 package (unchanged since v0.1.1), its tooling, and the
discoveries registry (133 entries), archived for reference. Everything was produced by AI agents (Anthropic's Claude)
under the direction of Timothy Seifert. Nothing has been reviewed by a human mathematician. Most new results are
computed modulo primes and to finite order; labels are given in each registry entry. Nothing here is a proof or a
disproof of the Hodge conjecture.

**Main addition: an open lead on cubic tenfolds** (registry O-TENFOLD-CONE-LEAD; readable summary
leads/TENFOLD_CONE_LEAD_DOSSIER.md). It is an open lead, NOT a candidate: there is no specific smooth member with a
proof that the class is Hodge and no known cycle, and the evidence is finite-order and mostly mod p.
- At the Fermat cubic tenfold with Movasati's two 5-planes meeting in a P^2, the class [P1] - [P2] (lambda = -1) has
  rank-1 first-order directions that extend as formal arcs (one to order 12 at two primes; about 70 more to orders
  9-10; none obstructed), while lambda = 2 is obstructed at order 6 (exact certificate for three directions) and 26
  other ratios are obstructed at order 6.
- A pre-registered test at points without the swap symmetry of the planes gives the same pattern (blind-checked,
  mod p). At points of a C_18 family F_T the class of a cone cycle has one extra Hodge-tangent direction,
  unobstructed at orders 2 (blind-checked) and 3 (mod p).
- A pre-registered search among six named construction types found none that explains the codimension-54 branch
  (N-TENFOLD-CODIM54-SEARCH). This rules out only those types.
- lambda_0 = +1 for this configuration (D-TENFOLD-LAMBDA0-CHECK): lambda = -1 is not the special coefficient of the
  general rule.
