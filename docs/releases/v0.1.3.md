# HodgeLociCI v0.1.3 (25 November 2026): release notes

**What this is.** A snapshot of the HodgeLociCI Macaulay2 package (unchanged since v0.1.1), its tooling, and the
discoveries registry (142 entries), archived for reference. Everything was produced by AI agents (Anthropic's Claude)
under the direction of Timothy Seifert. Nothing has been reviewed by a human mathematician. Most new results are
computed modulo primes and to finite order, and several steps are paper arguments written by an AI. Labels are given
in each registry entry. Nothing here is a proof or a disproof of the Hodge conjecture.

**Main addition: a candidate explanation of the open lead on cubic tenfolds.** The lead (registry
O-TENFOLD-CONE-LEAD) stays OPEN until a mathematician has read the arguments. Readable write-up:
leads/TENFOLD_COBLE_LEAD.md.

- **The question.** For Movasati's two 5-planes meeting in a P^2 on cubic tenfolds, the class with coefficient -1
  appeared to stay Hodge on a region W of codimension 54, one dimension larger than the C_18 family F_T (codimension
  55) and not explained by any construction tried (v0.1.2; N-TENFOLD-CODIM54-SEARCH, -SEARCH-2, N-TENFOLD-DIRECT-METHODS,
  N-TENFOLD-SINGULAR-SECTIONS).
- **The candidate (D-TENFOLD-COBLE-FAMILY; MOD p at two primes, exact over QQ at two points, blind-checked mod p).**
  An explicit family of cubic tenfolds whose P^8-section is a Coble cubic, the cubic sevenfold singular along an
  abelian surface (Coble 1917; see Beauville 2003 and Benedetti-Manivel-Tanturri, arXiv:1904.10848). It has
  codimension 54 and three moduli against F_T's two; its boundary, where the abelian surface degenerates to an
  elliptic scroll, is F_T. At a general member the first-order Hodge locus of the class equals the family's tangent
  space, and along F_T the family leaves in exactly the extra direction.
- **The cycle (D-TENFOLD-COBLE-CYCLE-CLASS; paper argument by an AI, mod p inputs).** Hyperplane sections through the
  P^8 have 72 nodes with one relation. A 5-fold swept by 4-planes over a theta curve of the abelian surface has
  self-intersection -144, where a multiple of h^5 would give 48; if the argument holds, the class is algebraic on the
  whole family (the sign of one coefficient is undetermined).
- **Higher order (D-TENFOLD-COBLE-ARC-ORDERS; MOD p).** Orders 2 and 3 of the Hodge condition hold exactly along the
  explicit arc at a point of F_T.
- **A control on the known coefficient +1 (D-TENFOLD-PFAFFIAN-K3; MOD p).** There the P^8-section is a Pfaffian cubic
  singular along a K3 surface of genus 8 (56 nodes, one relation), and the same self-intersection ladder reproduces
  known lattice numbers. Observation (not a theorem): the two special coefficients correspond to the two kinds of
  surface with trivial canonical class.

**Not established.** A mathematician's reading of the two paper arguments (local classes at the nodes; the ladder of
self-intersections); exact arithmetic for the node counts; the sign; a blind check of order 3; that the family is the
component W seen at the Fermat point (argued, not shown).
