Hodge loci: where the agents are

A live view of an AI-agent research project on Hodge loci (higher-dimensional Noether–Lefschetz loci) of combinations of linear and complete-intersection cycles in smooth hypersurfaces: what has been found, what was ruled out, and what is open. Built from the project's discoveries registry and its Macaulay2 package HodgeLociCI.

Read this first. Everything here was produced by AI agents (Anthropic's Claude) under the direction of Timothy Seifert and checked only by other AI agents. No human mathematician has reviewed it. Published papers were read by machine. Every entry carries a status label; an entry is exactly as strong as its label. This is not a list of established theorems, and nothing here is, or bears on, a proof or disproof of the Hodge conjecture.
